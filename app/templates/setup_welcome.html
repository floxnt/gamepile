Welcome to GamePile

GamePile manages your Steam backlog and helps you decide what to play. Before we can read your library, we need two things from Steam:

  • A Steam Web API key — a free read-only token tied to your account.
  • Your SteamID — your account's unique identifier.

Both values are stored in your operating system's secure keychain (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain / Linux Secret Service) and never leave your machine. GamePile is a local-only desktop app — there's no server, no account, and no telemetry.
