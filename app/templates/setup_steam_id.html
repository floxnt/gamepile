Either a numeric SteamID or your vanity URL works.

  • Numeric SteamID64 — a 17-digit number, e.g. 76561198000000000

  • Profile URL — paste steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198…

Find your profile by opening Steam → your name (top right) → profile. The URL bar shows one of the formats above.
